# MergeBar Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for MergeBar macOS Application
**Effective Date:** 20 June, 2025

Thank you for using MergeBar! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data in relation to our macOS application available on the Mac App Store. Please read it carefully to understand our commitment to your privacy.

---

### 1. Information Collection

MergeBar is designed with privacy in mind. The app does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or any third party. There is no account registration required beyond authenticating with your GitLab account. We do not collect or access any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email, or contact details.

---

### 2. GitLab Authentication & Keychain

MergeBar requires a **GitLab personal access token** to access your merge requests, issues, and to-do items. This token is stored **locally and securely** using the **macOS Keychain**, which encrypts the data and protects it with system-level security.

When communicating with the GitLab API, your token is sent in the **Authorization header** of HTTPS requests as a **private API token**. These requests are made directly from your device to GitLab over a secure, encrypted connection (TLS/SSL). The token is never sent to or processed by any server we control.

---

### 3. Use of Information

MergeBar uses the GitLab token **only** to retrieve and display your GitLab data (merge requests, issues, and to-dos). No information is sent to us, stored remotely, or used for analytics, tracking, or advertising. All processing happens locally on your Mac.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

MergeBar does not integrate with any third-party services for analytics, advertising, or data collection. However, it does communicate with **GitLab’s API**, and your interactions with GitLab are governed by [GitLab’s Privacy Policy](https://about.gitlab.com/privacy/). We recommend reviewing their policy to understand how your data is managed on their platform.

---

### 5. Data Security

MergeBar uses Apple's Keychain service to protect your GitLab token. Communications with GitLab are secured via HTTPS using industry-standard encryption protocols. We do not access or transmit your token to any service other than GitLab. We take reasonable measures to safeguard your data on your device.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Any changes will be published within the application or the App Store listing. Continued use of MergeBar after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
